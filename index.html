Australia Energy Trade Analysis (1989–2024)

Tableau Visual Analytics Energy / Supply Chain Forecasting

Problem: Where is the structural risk in Australia's energy trade — and what does the next five years look like?

Explored 35 years of Australian energy import/export data to surface a critical asymmetry: Australia is a major energy exporter (coal, gas) yet critically dependent on imported petroleum, which accounts for 97%+ of energy imports in 2023. Built three analytical layers — raw dollar values, compositional ratios, and year-on-year change — before visualizing in Tableau.

Four global crises left measurable marks in the data: the GFC (2008), Oil Price Crash (2016), COVID-19 (2020), and the Russia-Ukraine War (2022). The project includes a 5-year petroleum import forecast (2025–2029) with 95% confidence intervals.
